Colleague Zero · What’s needed

Everything you need, before you build.

What to sort out before Friday, what to have ready at kickoff, and what to hand in on Saturday.

Apply byMon 9 Nov
TeamSolo or up to 5

01Step 1

Before Friday

By 13 Nov
  1. Apply by Monday 9 NovemberEnter on your own or as a team of up to five. Apply now
  2. Join the Melsoft Academy DiscordThe invite is in your welcome email. Meet the other builders, find teammates and catch every announcement.
  3. Watch your inbox for the join linkFriday is online. We email it before kickoff on Friday 13 November at 09:30 SAST.
  4. Pick an education problemA real problem that learners, facilitators or education teams face every day. You can change your idea on the day.
02Step 2

Your setup

Ready at kickoff
  1. A laptop you can build onOne where you can install tools and run code.
  2. Stable internet on FridayFriday is online, from the 09:30 opening to the Loom report at midnight.
  3. Your laptop and charger in Rosebank on SaturdaySaturday is in person at WeWork The Link, 173 Oxford Rd, Rosebank, Johannesburg. Check-in opens at 09:00.
  4. Access to the AI tools you plan to useSet up your accounts and API keys and test them before kickoff, so you spend the weekend building, not signing up.
  5. A GitHub accountYour code goes in a repository that you share with the judges.
  6. A microphone, and a camera if you have oneFor Friday’s online idea pitches and progress updates.
03Step 3

What to submit

Sat 14 Nov · 11:00
  1. A Loom progress report by midnight on FridayEvery participant records one: your individual or team progress, the work completed and what’s still outstanding.
  2. A link to your code repositoryShared so the judges can open it.
  3. A README in the repositoryThe problem you chose, which agent does what, and how to run it.

That’s all you hand in. Everything else happens in person at 12:00.

04Step 4

What you present

Sat 14 Nov · 12:00
  1. The problem you chose
  2. A live demo of your digital colleague at work
  3. How your agents reason and correct themselves
  4. What you would build next
