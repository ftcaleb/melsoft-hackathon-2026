Step 1

Before Friday

By 13 Nov
  1. Apply by Monday 9 NovemberEnter on your own or as a team of up to five. Apply now
  2. Join the Melsoft Academy DiscordThe invite is in your welcome email. Meet the other builders, find teammates and catch every announcement.
  3. Watch your inbox for the join linkWe email it before kickoff on Friday 13 November at 11:00 SAST.
  4. Pick an education problemA real problem that learners, facilitators or education teams face every day. You can change your idea on the day.
Step 2

Your setup

Ready at kickoff
  1. A laptop you can build onOne where you can install tools and run code.
  2. Stable internet for both daysEverything happens online, from kickoff on Friday to the winner on Saturday.
  3. Access to the AI tools you plan to useSet up your accounts and API keys and test them before kickoff, so you spend the weekend building, not signing up.
  4. A GitHub accountYour code goes in a repository that you share with the judges.
  5. A microphone, and a camera if you have oneFor the pitches, demos and live presentations.
Step 3

What to submit

Sat 14 Nov · 11:00
  1. A link to your code repositoryShared so the judges can open it.
  2. A README in the repositoryThe problem you chose, which agent does what, and how to run it.

That’s all you hand in. Everything else happens live at 12:00.

Step 4

What you present

Sat 14 Nov · 12:00
  1. The problem you chose
  2. A live demo of your digital colleague at work
  3. How your agents reason and correct themselves
  4. What you would build next
