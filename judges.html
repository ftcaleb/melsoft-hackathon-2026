How you’ll be judged

Five things a strong project does.

Use these as your build checklist. The best digital colleagues do all five.

Judging criteria05
  1. 01/05

    It reasons.

    Breaks a goal into steps and decides what to do next, rather than following a fixed script.

  2. 02/05

    It self-corrects.

    Reviews its own work, catches errors and tries again until the result is right.

  3. 03/05

    It executes.

    Completes a multi-step workflow and delivers a finished result, not a suggestion.

  4. 04/05

    It collaborates.

    Uses more than one agent, each with a clear role, handing work to one another.

  5. 05/05

    It serves education.

    Solves a specific, real need in teaching, learning or running an education programme.
